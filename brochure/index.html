Why you should discover it

A sharp is not a flat.

Your ear has always known it. Most digital instruments give D♯ and E♭ one and the same key. Music Motion gives the difference back — and lets you hear it.

C♯4 · PYTHAGOREAN
278.44 Hz
D♭4 · PYTHAGOREAN
274.69 Hz
BOTH · EQUAL TEMPERAMENT
277.18 Hz

Two notes with two names — and on the piano, one compromise between them. A4 = 440 Hz.

The idea

Music tuned by ear,
not by formulas.

Everything in Music Motion rests on one base: pure Pythagorean tuning — ratios of 2 and 3, that is, pure octaves and pure fifths, without the rounding of equal temperament. The ear is the judge.

It is built for gradual immersion. Tuning, writing, interpretation, accompaniment, orchestration: each can be approached step by step, at the pace of someone learning alone or of someone who teaches.

Play it right away

Your computer keyboard
is the instrument.

Open the Cosmic Keyboard in your browser and play — QWERTY, AZERTY or QWERTZ, it plays by the position of the key. Each key is split: pure Pythagorean pitch on top, an equal-tempered strip below, so you can compare them side by side. More than sixty instruments, all Pythagorean — the piano included.

Who it is for

For anyone —
and for the classroom too.

If you are starting

The composition game of the Coach starts from a model melody and ends with a four-voice piece written by you — without having studied theory first.

If you play

Study your part with the accompaniment, record yourself over it with the microphone, and keep the take as a WAV to share with your teacher.

If you teach

Write your own exercises — instrumental practice, harmony, counterpoint, composition — and your students work through them hearing the most natural intonation. Music schools and conservatoires have a programme of their own.
